## Setup Your Account

<Steps>
  <Step title="Create Account">
    Open the app and sign up using your email or social media accounts. You can also connect directly with your Web3 wallet.
  </Step>
  
  <Step title="Enable Location Services">
    Grant location permissions to discover rocks near you. Don't worry—your privacy is protected, and location data is only used for gameplay.
  </Step>
  
  <Step title="Complete Tutorial">
    Follow the in-app tutorial to collect your first rock and learn the basics of the game.
  </Step>
  
  <Step title="Connect Wallet (Optional)">
    To mint NFTs and participate in the play-and-earn economy, connect a compatible Web3 wallet like MetaMask or WalletConnect.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Collect Your First Rock

<Note>
  Rocks appear at various real-world locations. Open your map to see nearby rocks and start exploring!
</Note>

### Finding Rocks

1. **Open the Map View** - Tap the map icon to see rocks in your area
2. **Navigate to Location** - Follow the GPS directions to reach a rock
3. **Collect the Rock** - When you're within range, tap the rock to collect it
4. **View Your Collection** - Check your inventory to see your new rock

### Rock Properties

Each rock has unique properties:

- **Rarity Level** - Common, Uncommon, Rare, Epic, Legendary
- **Element Type** - Fire, Water, Earth, Air, Cosmic
- **Power Level** - Determines evolution potential
- **Location Origin** - Where the rock was discovered

## Mint Your First NFT

<Warning>
  You'll need a small amount of cryptocurrency (gas fees) to mint your first NFT. Make sure your wallet is funded before proceeding.
</Warning>

<Steps>
  <Step title="Select a Rock">
    Go to your inventory and choose a rock you want to mint as an NFT
  </Step>
  
  <Step title="Review Details">
    Check the rock's properties and estimated minting cost
  </Step>
  
  <Step title="Confirm Transaction">
    Approve the transaction in your connected wallet
  </Step>
  
  <Step title="Wait for Confirmation">
    The minting process typically takes 30-60 seconds. You'll receive a notification when complete.
  </Step>
</Steps>
