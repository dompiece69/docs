## Setup Guide

### MetaMask Setup

<Steps>
  <Step title="Install MetaMask">
    Download from [metamask.io](https://metamask.io) or your mobile app store
  </Step>
  
  <Step title="Create Wallet">
    Follow the setup wizard to create a new wallet or import existing one
  </Step>
  
  <Step title="Secure Your Wallet">
    Write down your seed phrase and store it securely offline
  </Step>
  
  <Step title="Add Networks">
    Add Polygon, Base, or other supported networks to MetaMask
  </Step>
  
  <Step title="Connect to Space Rocks">
    Open Space Rocks app and tap "Connect Wallet" → Select MetaMask
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Connecting Your Wallet

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open App">
    Launch Space Rocks and navigate to Settings or Profile
  </Step>
  
  <Step title="Tap Connect Wallet">
    Select "Connect Wallet" button
  </Step>
  
  <Step title="Choose Wallet">
    Select your preferred wallet from the list
  </Step>
  
  <Step title="Approve Connection">
    Approve the connection request in your wallet
  </Step>
  
  <Step title="Verify Connection">
    Your wallet address will appear in the app
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Security Best Practices

<Warning>
  **Never share your:**
  - Seed phrase / Recovery phrase
  - Private keys
  - Wallet passwords
  
  Space Rocks will NEVER ask for these!
</Warning>

### Wallet Security Tips

- ✅ Use hardware wallets for large holdings
- ✅ Enable biometric authentication
- ✅ Keep software updated
- ✅ Use strong, unique passwords
- ✅ Enable 2FA where available
- ✅ Verify all transactions before signing
- ❌ Never screenshot seed phrases
- ❌ Don't store seeds digitally
- ❌ Don't share wallet details publicly
