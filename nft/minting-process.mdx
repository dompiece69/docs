## What is NFT Minting?

Minting converts your in-game rocks into Non-Fungible Tokens (NFTs) on the blockchain, giving you true ownership and the ability to trade them on decentralized marketplaces.

## Prerequisites

Before minting your first NFT, you'll need:

<Steps>
  <Step title="Web3 Wallet">
    Install and set up a compatible wallet like MetaMask, Trust Wallet, or WalletConnect
  </Step>
  
  <Step title="Cryptocurrency">
    Have enough cryptocurrency to cover gas fees (typically $2-10 depending on network congestion)
  </Step>
  
  <Step title="Connected Wallet">
    Connect your wallet to the Space Rocks app
  </Step>
  
  <Step title="Collected Rock">
    Have at least one rock in your inventory to mint
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Minting Process

### Step-by-Step Guide

<Steps>
  <Step title="Select Rock">
    Go to your inventory and tap the rock you want to mint. Review its properties and estimated value.
  </Step>
  
  <Step title="Choose Blockchain">
    Select which blockchain network to mint on. Consider gas fees and your intended use.
  </Step>
  
  <Step title="Review Details">
    Check the minting summary:
    - Rock properties and metadata
    - Estimated gas fees
    - Meme coin backing amount
    - Total cost
  </Step>
  
  <Step title="Confirm Transaction">
    Tap "Mint NFT" and approve the transaction in your wallet. This may take a few seconds.
  </Step>
  
  <Step title="Wait for Confirmation">
    The blockchain processes your transaction (30 seconds to 5 minutes depending on network).
  </Step>
  
  <Step title="Receive NFT">
    Once confirmed, your NFT appears in your wallet and can be viewed on blockchain explorers.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Batch Minting

Mint multiple rocks at once to save on gas fees:

### How It Works

<Steps>
  <Step title="Select Multiple Rocks">
    Choose up to 10 rocks from your inventory to mint together
  </Step>
  
  <Step title="Review Batch">
    See total costs and estimated gas savings (typically 40-60% cheaper per NFT)
  </Step>
  
  <Step title="Confirm Transaction">
    Approve one transaction that mints all selected rocks
  </Step>
  
  <Step title="Receive All NFTs">
    All NFTs are minted in a single transaction
  </Step>
</Steps>

### Batch Minting Benefits

- **Gas Savings**: 40-60% cheaper per NFT
- **Time Efficient**: One transaction instead of many
- **Bulk Discounts**: 10% off platform fees
- **Priority Processing**: Faster confirmation times

<Tip>
  Save your rocks and batch mint 10 at a time to maximize savings!
</Tip>

## Lazy Minting

Delay blockchain minting until sale:

### What is Lazy Minting?

- List rocks on marketplace without minting
- Buyer pays gas fees when purchasing
- You save upfront costs
- NFT is minted upon first sale

## NFT Standards

Space Rocks uses industry-standard NFT protocols:

### ERC-721

Our primary NFT standard:

- **Standard**: ERC-721 (Ethereum standard)
- **Compatibility**: Works with all major wallets and marketplaces
- **Features**: Unique, non-fungible tokens
- **Use Case**: Individual rock NFTs

### ERC-1155

For special collections:

- **Standard**: ERC-1155 (Multi-token standard)
- **Compatibility**: Advanced wallet support
- **Features**: Batch transfers, lower gas fees
- **Use Case**: Event rocks, limited editions

## Minting Strategies

### When to Mint

<Tip>
  **Optimal Minting Times:**
  - During low network congestion (weekends, late night)
  - When gas prices are below average
  - Before listing on marketplace
  - After evolving to higher rarity
  - During minting discount events
</Tip>

### What to Mint

Prioritize minting:

1. **Legendary and Mythic rocks** - Highest value
2. **Shiny variants** - Collector's items
3. **Landmark rocks** - Location significance
4. **Event exclusives** - Limited availability
5. **High power rocks** - Better stats
6. **Complete sets** - Collection value

### What Not to Mint

Avoid minting:

- Common rocks (unless batch minting)
- Low power rocks
- Rocks you plan to use as evolution fuel
- Rocks during high gas fee periods
- Rocks you're unsure about keeping

## Security Best Practices

<Warning>
  **Important Security Tips:**
  - Never share your seed phrase
  - Always verify contract addresses
  - Use hardware wallets for valuable NFTs
  - Enable 2FA on your accounts
  - Be cautious of phishing attempts
</Warning>

### Protecting Your NFTs

- **Hardware Wallets**: Store valuable NFTs on Ledger or Trezor
- **Multi-Sig Wallets**: Require multiple approvals for transfers
- **Cold Storage**: Keep long-term holdings offline
- **Regular Backups**: Backup wallet seed phrases securely
- **Revoke Approvals**: Regularly check and revoke unnecessary contract approvals
