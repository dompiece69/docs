## Marketplace Overview

The Space Rocks Marketplace is your hub for trading NFTs with other players. Buy rare rocks, sell your collection, or trade to complete sets.

## Buying NFTs

<Steps>
  <Step title="Browse Listings">
    Explore available NFTs using filters for rarity, element, price, and more
  </Step>
  
  <Step title="Select NFT">
    Click on any listing to view detailed information and seller history
  </Step>
  
  <Step title="Make Purchase">
    Click "Buy Now" or place a bid if it's an auction
  </Step>
  
  <Step title="Confirm Transaction">
    Approve the transaction in your wallet
  </Step>
  
  <Step title="Receive NFT">
    The NFT is transferred to your wallet immediately
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Selling NFTs

### Fixed Price Listings

<Steps>
  <Step title="Select NFT">
    Choose an NFT from your collection to sell
  </Step>
  
  <Step title="Set Price">
    Enter your desired price in meme coins or cryptocurrency
  </Step>
  
  <Step title="List Item">
    Confirm listing (small gas fee may apply)
  </Step>
  
  <Step title="Wait for Sale">
    Your NFT appears in marketplace listings
  </Step>
</Steps>

### Auction Listings

Create time-limited auctions:

- **Starting Bid**: Minimum bid amount
- **Reserve Price**: Optional minimum sale price
- **Duration**: 1 hour to 7 days
- **Buy Now Price**: Optional instant purchase price

## Marketplace Fees

| Transaction Type | Fee | Goes To |
|-----------------|-----|---------|
| Buying | 0% | N/A |
| Selling | 2.5% | Platform |
| Creator Royalty | 5% | Original minter |
| Legendary+ Sales | 0% | Fee waived |

<Note>
  Legendary and Mythic rock holders pay ZERO marketplace fees!
</Note>
