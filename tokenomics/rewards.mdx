## Reward Types

## Staking Rewards

### NFT Staking

Stake your NFTs to earn passive $ROCK:

| Rarity | Daily APY | Example (30 days) |
|--------|-----------|-------------------|
| Common | 0.5% | 10 $ROCK → 11.5 $ROCK |
| Uncommon | 1% | 50 $ROCK → 65 $ROCK |
| Rare | 2% | 200 $ROCK → 260 $ROCK |
| Epic | 4% | 1,000 $ROCK → 1,400 $ROCK |
| Legendary | 8% | 5,000 $ROCK → 7,400 $ROCK |
| Mythic | 15% | 25,000 $ROCK → 43,750 $ROCK |
