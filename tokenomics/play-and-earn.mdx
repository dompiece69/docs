## Earning Strategies

### Beginner Strategy (0-30 days)

**Focus**: Build collection and learn mechanics

<Steps>
  <Step title="Collect Daily">
    Collect 10-20 rocks per day in your area
    **Expected**: 50-100 $ROCK/day
  </Step>
  
  <Step title="Complete Quests">
    Finish all daily and weekly quests
    **Expected**: 100-200 $ROCK/day
  </Step>
  
  <Step title="Mint First NFTs">
    Mint your first rare+ rocks
    **Expected**: Start building staking portfolio
  </Step>
  
  <Step title="Join Community">
    Connect with other players for tips
    **Expected**: Learn optimal strategies
  </Step>
</Steps>

**Total Expected**: 150-300 $ROCK/day

### Intermediate Strategy (30-90 days)

**Focus**: Optimize collection and start staking

<Steps>
  <Step title="Route Optimization">
    Plan efficient collection routes
    **Expected**: 200-400 $ROCK/day from collection
  </Step>
  
  <Step title="Start Staking">
    Stake 5-10 rare+ NFTs
    **Expected**: 50-150 $ROCK/day passive
  </Step>
  
  <Step title="Evolution Focus">
    Evolve rocks to higher rarities
    **Expected**: Increase collection value
  </Step>
  
  <Step title="Marketplace Activity">
    Buy low, sell high
    **Expected**: 100-300 $ROCK/day profit
  </Step>
</Steps>

**Total Expected**: 350-850 $ROCK/day

### Advanced Strategy (90+ days)

**Focus**: Maximum earnings through optimization

<Steps>
  <Step title="Premium Staking">
    Stake legendary+ NFTs with long lock periods
    **Expected**: 500-2,000 $ROCK/day passive
  </Step>
  
  <Step title="Event Participation">
    Compete in all events and tournaments
    **Expected**: 1,000-5,000 $ROCK/week
  </Step>
  
  <Step title="Market Making">
    Provide liquidity and trade actively
    **Expected**: 500-1,500 $ROCK/day
  </Step>
  
  <Step title="Referral Network">
    Build active referral network
    **Expected**: 200-1,000 $ROCK/day
  </Step>
</Steps>

**Total Expected**: 2,000-10,000+ $ROCK/day
