## Game Overview

Space Rocks combines real-world exploration with blockchain technology to create a unique play-and-earn experience. Your goal is to explore locations, collect rocks, evolve them into powerful artifacts, and build a valuable NFT collection.

## Core Gameplay Loop

<Steps>
  <Step title="Explore">
    Travel to real-world locations to discover geo-located rocks
  </Step>
  
  <Step title="Collect">
    Gather rocks by physically visiting their locations
  </Step>
  
  <Step title="Mint">
    Transform collected rocks into NFTs on the blockchain
  </Step>
  
  <Step title="Evolve">
    Upgrade and combine rocks to increase their rarity and value
  </Step>
  
  <Step title="Earn">
    Generate rewards through staking, trading, and gameplay
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Exploration Mechanics

### Finding Rocks

Rocks spawn at various real-world locations based on several factors:

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Location Types" icon="map-pin">
    - **Urban Areas**: Higher spawn rates but more common rocks
    - **Parks & Nature**: Balanced spawn rates with varied rarity
    - **Landmarks**: Lower spawn rates but higher chance of rare rocks
    - **Remote Locations**: Very rare spawns with potential for legendary finds
  </Accordion>
  
  <Accordion title="Time-Based Spawning" icon="clock">
    - **Day/Night Cycle**: Different rocks appear at different times
    - **Weather Conditions**: Rain, snow, and other weather affects spawns
    - **Special Events**: Limited-time rocks during community events
  </Accordion>
  
  <Accordion title="Spawn Radius" icon="circle">
    You must be within **20 meters** of a rock to collect it. The app will vibrate and show a collection prompt when you're in range.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

### Map Interface

<Tip>
  Use the map filters to show only specific rarity levels or element types you're hunting for.
</Tip>

The in-game map displays:
- **Blue Markers**: Common rocks
- **Green Markers**: Uncommon rocks
- **Purple Markers**: Rare rocks
- **Orange Markers**: Epic rocks
- **Gold Markers**: Legendary rocks
- **Rainbow Markers**: Special event rocks

## Collection Strategy

### Energy System

You have a limited amount of **Energy** that regenerates over time:

- **Starting Energy**: 100 points
- **Collection Cost**: 10 energy per rock
- **Regeneration Rate**: 1 energy per 5 minutes
- **Max Energy**: 100 (can be increased with upgrades)

<Warning>
  Plan your exploration routes carefully to maximize collections before your energy runs out!
</Warning>

### Inventory Management

Your inventory has limited space:

| Account Level | Inventory Slots |
|--------------|----------------|
| Level 1-5    | 50 slots       |
| Level 6-10   | 100 slots      |
| Level 11-20  | 200 slots      |
| Level 21+    | 500 slots      |

<Note>
  Mint rocks as NFTs to free up inventory space while maintaining ownership.
</Note>

### Element Types

Rocks come in five elemental varieties:

- **🔥 Fire**: Bonus damage in battles, higher evolution success rate
- **💧 Water**: Increased healing properties, better staking rewards
- **🌍 Earth**: Enhanced durability, reduced evolution costs
- **💨 Air**: Faster energy regeneration, improved collection radius
- **✨ Cosmic**: Balanced stats, unique evolution paths

### Power Level

Power Level ranges from **1 to 1000** and affects:
- Evolution success rates
- Earning potential
- Battle performance (in PvP mode)
- Marketplace value

## Evolution System

Transform your rocks into more powerful forms through evolution.

### Evolution Requirements

To evolve a rock, you need:

1. **Base Rock**: The rock you want to evolve
2. **Catalyst Rocks**: Additional rocks of the same element (consumed in process)
3. **Evolution Stones**: Special items earned through gameplay
4. **Meme Coins**: Currency cost based on target rarity

### Evolution Paths

```
Simple Rock (Common)
    ↓
Polished Rock (Uncommon)
    ↓
Crystal Formation (Rare)
    ↓
Cosmic Artifact (Epic)
    ↓
Stellar Relic (Legendary)
    ↓
Quantum Entity (Mythic)
```

<Tip>
  Each evolution increases the rock's power level by 50-100 points and unlocks new visual effects!
</Tip>

## Play-and-Earn Mechanics

### Daily Rewards

Log in daily to claim rewards:

| Streak Days | Reward |
|------------|--------|
| Day 1-7    | 10-50 coins |
| Day 8-14   | 60-100 coins |
| Day 15-30  | 120-200 coins |
| Day 30+    | 250+ coins + bonus items |

### Staking

Stake your NFT rocks to earn passive income:

- **Common Rocks**: 0.5% daily return
- **Uncommon Rocks**: 1% daily return
- **Rare Rocks**: 2% daily return
- **Epic Rocks**: 4% daily return
- **Legendary Rocks**: 8% daily return
- **Mythic Rocks**: 15% daily return

<Warning>
  Staked rocks cannot be traded or evolved until unstaked. Unstaking has a 24-hour cooldown period.
</Warning>

### Quests & Achievements

Complete quests to earn bonus rewards:

- **Daily Quests**: Collect 5 rocks, walk 1km, mint 1 NFT
- **Weekly Quests**: Collect 50 rocks, evolve 3 rocks, trade on marketplace
- **Monthly Quests**: Reach new locations, complete collections, participate in events

### Referral Program

Invite friends to earn rewards:

- **5% of friend's earnings** for the first 30 days
- **Bonus rewards** when friends reach milestones
- **Exclusive NFTs** for top referrers

## Advanced Strategies

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Optimal Collection Routes" icon="route">
    Plan circular routes that maximize rock density while minimizing travel distance. Use the route planner feature to optimize your exploration.
  </Accordion>
  
  <Accordion title="Market Timing" icon="chart-line">
    Rock prices fluctuate based on supply and demand. Monitor the marketplace to buy low and sell high. Legendary rocks typically peak in value during weekend events.
  </Accordion>
  
  <Accordion title="Evolution Strategy" icon="flask">
    Don't evolve every rock immediately. Sometimes it's more profitable to sell multiple common rocks than to evolve them into one rare rock.
  </Accordion>
  
  <Accordion title="Element Synergy" icon="atom">
    Build collections focused on specific elements to unlock set bonuses and special abilities in future game modes.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
