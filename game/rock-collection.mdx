## Collection Basics

Collecting rocks is the core gameplay mechanic of Space Rocks. Each rock you collect has unique properties and potential value as an NFT.

## Collection Process

### Step-by-Step Guide

<Steps>
  <Step title="Locate a Rock">
    Open your map to see nearby rocks. Blue markers indicate common rocks, while gold markers show legendary finds.
  </Step>
  
  <Step title="Navigate to Location">
    Use the in-app navigation or compass mode to reach the rock's location. You must be within 20 meters to collect.
  </Step>
  
  <Step title="Verify Energy">
    Ensure you have at least 10 energy points. If not, wait for regeneration or use an energy refill item.
  </Step>
  
  <Step title="Collect the Rock">
    Tap the "Collect" button when in range. The app will verify your location and deduct 10 energy.
  </Step>
  
  <Step title="View Rock Details">
    After collection, view the rock's properties including rarity, element, power level, and special attributes.
  </Step>
</Steps>

### Collection Requirements

| Requirement | Details |
|------------|---------|
| **Energy Cost** | 10 energy per rock |
| **Distance** | Within 20 meters of rock |
| **Inventory Space** | At least 1 free slot |
| **Account Status** | Active (not suspended) |
| **GPS Accuracy** | Better than 20 meters |

## Rock Properties

Every collected rock has multiple attributes:

### Core Attributes

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Rarity Level" icon="star">
    Determines base value and evolution potential:
    - **Common**: 60% drop rate
    - **Uncommon**: 25% drop rate
    - **Rare**: 10% drop rate
    - **Epic**: 4% drop rate
    - **Legendary**: 0.9% drop rate
    - **Mythic**: 0.1% drop rate
  </Accordion>
  
  <Accordion title="Element Type" icon="atom">
    Five elemental categories with unique bonuses:
    - **🔥 Fire**: Combat bonuses, evolution success boost
    - **💧 Water**: Staking rewards, healing properties
    - **🌍 Earth**: Durability, reduced costs
    - **💨 Air**: Energy regeneration, collection radius
    - **✨ Cosmic**: Balanced stats, unique evolutions
  </Accordion>
  
  <Accordion title="Power Level" icon="bolt">
    Ranges from 1-1000, affects:
    - Evolution success rates
    - Earning potential
    - Battle performance
    - Market value
  </Accordion>
  
  <Accordion title="Special Traits" icon="sparkles">
    Rare additional properties:
    - **Shiny**: 1% chance, +50% value
    - **Ancient**: 0.5% chance, unique appearance
    - **Charged**: 2% chance, faster evolution
    - **Blessed**: 0.1% chance, double rewards
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

### Location Metadata

Each rock records where it was found:

- **GPS Coordinates**: Exact collection location
- **Location Name**: City, landmark, or area name
- **Collection Time**: Date and time of discovery
- **Weather Conditions**: Weather at time of collection
- **Collector ID**: Your unique player identifier

<Note>
  Location metadata becomes part of the NFT and can increase value for rocks collected at famous landmarks or during special events.
</Note>

## Inventory Management

### Inventory System

Your inventory has limited space that expands as you level up:

```
Level 1-5:   50 slots
Level 6-10:  100 slots
Level 11-20: 200 slots
Level 21+:   500 slots
```

### Inventory Actions

<Tabs>
  <Tab title="View Details">

    - Full attribute list

    - Minting cost

  </Tab>

## Collection Strategies

### Maximizing Efficiency

<Tip>

1. **Cluster Collection**: Focus on areas with multiple rocks

## Advanced Collection Features

### Auto-Collection

Unlock auto-collection at Level 20:

- Automatically collect rocks within 10 meters
- Costs 15 energy instead of 10
- Can be toggled on/off
- Respects inventory limits
- Notification for each collection

### Collection Multipliers

Boost your collection efficiency:

| Multiplier | Effect | Duration | Cost |
|-----------|--------|----------|------|
| 2x Energy | Half energy cost | 1 hour | 100 coins |
| 2x Radius | Double collection range | 1 hour | 150 coins |
| 2x Rarity | Double rare chance | 30 min | 500 coins |
| 3x Rewards | Triple collection rewards | 15 min | 1,000 coins |
