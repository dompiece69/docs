## Overview

Space Rocks uses advanced GPS technology to create a seamless connection between the physical and digital worlds. Rocks spawn at real-world coordinates, encouraging players to explore their surroundings and discover new locations.

## How It Works

### GPS Integration

The game uses your device's GPS to:

- **Track Your Position**: Real-time location updates with 5-10 meter accuracy
- **Detect Nearby Rocks**: Automatic scanning within a 500-meter radius
- **Verify Collection**: Ensure you're physically present at rock locations
- **Record Discovery**: Log unique locations you've visited

<Note>
  Space Rocks works best with GPS enabled and a clear view of the sky. Indoor locations may have reduced accuracy.
</Note>

### Weather Effects

Real-world weather affects rock spawning:

- **☀️ Sunny**: Fire rocks +30%, Water rocks -20%
- **🌧️ Rainy**: Water rocks +30%, Fire rocks -20%
- **❄️ Snowy**: Ice variant rocks appear, +50% rare chance
- **⛈️ Stormy**: Cosmic rocks +50%, special lightning variants
- **🌫️ Foggy**: Mysterious rocks appear, unknown properties until collected

## Collection Mechanics

### Collection Range

<Warning>
  You must be within **20 meters** of a rock to collect it. Spoofing or cheating will result in account suspension.
</Warning>

The collection process:

<Steps>
  <Step title="Detection">
    App detects you're within 50 meters of a rock and shows notification
  </Step>
  
  <Step title="Approach">
    Navigate to within 20 meters using the in-app compass
  </Step>
  
  <Step title="Verification">
    System verifies your GPS coordinates and movement patterns
  </Step>
  
  <Step title="Collection">
    Tap the rock to collect it (costs 10 energy)
  </Step>
  
  <Step title="Confirmation">
    Rock is added to your inventory with timestamp and location data
  </Step>
</Steps>

### Anti-Cheat System

Space Rocks employs sophisticated anti-cheat measures:

- **Movement Pattern Analysis**: Detects unnatural movement
- **Speed Limits**: Flags collections made while traveling too fast
- **Location Verification**: Cross-references multiple GPS sources
- **Behavioral Analysis**: Identifies bot-like collection patterns

<Warning>
  **Penalties for Cheating:**
  - First offense: 7-day suspension
  - Second offense: 30-day suspension + forfeit of earnings
  - Third offense: Permanent ban
</Warning>
