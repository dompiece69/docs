## Rarity System Overview

## Special Rarity Variants

### Shiny Variants

Any rock can appear as a shiny variant:

- **Occurrence**: 1% chance on any rock
- **Visual**: Alternate color scheme with sparkle effects
- **Value**: +50% base value
- **Collectibility**: Highly sought by collectors
- **Evolution**: Maintains shiny status through evolution

### Ancient Variants

Rare historical rocks:

- **Occurrence**: 0.5% chance on rare+ rocks
- **Visual**: Weathered appearance with runes
- **Value**: +100% base value
- **Special**: Unique lore and backstory
- **Evolution**: Unlocks special evolution paths

### Charged Variants

Energy-infused rocks:

- **Occurrence**: 2% chance on any rock
- **Visual**: Electric aura and crackling effects
- **Benefit**: +15% evolution success rate
- **Special**: Faster evolution times
- **Evolution**: Transfers charge to evolved form

### Blessed Variants

Divinely touched rocks:

- **Occurrence**: 0.1% chance on legendary+ rocks
- **Visual**: Holy glow and divine particles
- **Benefit**: Double all rewards
- **Special**: Cannot fail evolution
- **Evolution**: Guaranteed mythic evolution

## Investment Guide by Rarity

### Common Rocks
- **Investment**: Low risk, low reward
- **Strategy**: Mass collect for evolution fuel
- **ROI**: 10-20% through evolution
- **Time Horizon**: Days to weeks

### Uncommon Rocks
- **Investment**: Low-medium risk, medium reward
- **Strategy**: Hold for staking or evolve to rare
- **ROI**: 20-40% through staking/evolution
- **Time Horizon**: Weeks to months

### Rare Rocks
- **Investment**: Medium risk, medium-high reward
- **Strategy**: Long-term staking or marketplace trading
- **ROI**: 50-100% through staking/trading
- **Time Horizon**: Months

### Epic Rocks
- **Investment**: Medium-high risk, high reward
- **Strategy**: Premium staking or hold for appreciation
- **ROI**: 100-300% through staking/appreciation
- **Time Horizon**: Months to year

### Legendary Rocks
- **Investment**: High risk, very high reward
- **Strategy**: Long-term hold or competitive trading
- **ROI**: 300-1000% through appreciation
- **Time Horizon**: Year+

### Mythic Rocks
- **Investment**: Very high risk, extreme reward
- **Strategy**: Ultimate long-term investment
- **ROI**: 1000%+ potential
- **Time Horizon**: Multi-year
