## What is Space Rocks?

Space Rocks is a revolutionary **play-and-earn mobile game** that bridges the physical and digital worlds. Explore real-world locations to discover geo-located rocks, mint them as NFTs, and watch them evolve into rare cosmic artifacts backed by our native meme coin.
